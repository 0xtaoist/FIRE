# Landing v4: lead with the movement, prove it with Vlad

Written 2026-08-24. Synthesises Sonder's 2026-08-22 review with the Tenev clip

## The one line this rests on

Sonder's sharpest observation, and the thesis the page should be built to prove:

> **Most movement coins are built on a feeling. This one is built on a strategy
> people already follow with real money.**

Everything below is in service of that sentence.

## The rhyme

The FIRE movement's whole strategy: **buy assets and don't sell.**
$FIRE's whole mechanic: **buy the asset and don't sell.**

Nothing else in crypto has a movement whose real-world practice matches the
token mechanic this exactly. That is not a marketing angle, it is a structural
fact, and it is currently the last thing a visitor learns instead of the first.

## What is actually wrong, and it is cheaper to fix than it looks

The movement material **already exists and is good**. `MovementName` (the
acronym beat, FIRE spelled out of the movement's own four words) and
`MovementScale` (how big it already is, with the subreddit counts) are written,
designed and working.

They mount at **lines 1447 and 1448 of a 1484-line page.** They are the last
thing before the footer.

So the single highest-value change is not a rewrite. It is a **reorder**. The
page currently opens on a counter and a positioning line and asks the visitor to
work out what FIRE is; it should open by telling them, in language millions of
people already use.

## The order

Premise, identity, proof, receipts, mechanics. Each beat earns the next.

Note what moves rather than what is written. Beats 1, 4, 5 and 6 already exist.
Only 2 and 3 are new, and both are short.

## Beat 3: how Vlad is used, and how he is not

The clip is the strongest asset FIRE has. It is also the easiest thing on this
page to get legally and reputationally wrong, so the rule comes before the copy.

**He never says FIRE.** He describes the category. The only defensible claim is
that he is describing the mechanic we built, and the visitor connects it
themselves.

Nothing may read as endorsed by, partnered with, featured by, or approved by
Robinhood or Vlad Tenev. All are false. The page already carries an unaffiliated
disclaimer on the communities section and that instinct must survive into v4,
extended to cover this.

What the beat should do: show the quote, attributed in full, and let it sit.

> "...all of the developers have created all kinds of unique pools that we
> didn't even think would exist. And in all kinds of protocols where, for
> example, **if you hold a meme coin you just get stock tokens airdropped to
> you**, right?"
>
> Vlad Tenev, CEO, Robinhood

Then one quiet line underneath, in the page's own voice, doing no more than
pointing:

> That is the entire mechanic of $FIRE.

No "as seen on". No logos. No claim of a relationship. The restraint is what
makes it land: a testimonial is something you solicited, and this is not one.

**Placement is deliberate.** Vlad is a proof, not a premise. "Hold a meme coin,
get stock tokens" means nothing to someone who does not yet know what FIRE is or
why holding matters, so the movement has to come first. Put him third and he
answers the question the first two beats create, which is "is this real".

## Beat 2: the pivot, and the headline problem

Sonder: *"The headline text feels a bit templated."* Agreed, and the reason is
that the current hero leads with a category ("the RWA retention layer for
Robinhood Chain") rather than a claim. Retention layer is what FIRE is to the
chain. It is not what FIRE is to a person.

To a person it is: **you already believe in buying and not selling. This pays
you for it, in stocks.**

Beat 2 is one screen and should be close to bare. The movement's strategy on one
side, the token's mechanic on the other, in the same words, so the reader sees
the match rather than being told about it.

## What Sonder asked for that is not a reorder

- **Hero must show what $FIRE does: get paid in stocks for holding.** Covered by
  beats 1 and 2 landing before anything else.
- **Hero needs the proof points: first-payout wallets, all-time paid, the reward
  mechanic.** These exist. They move from the hero into beat 4, immediately after
  Vlad, where they read as evidence rather than as an opening statistic. A number
  with no premise in front of it is decoration; the same number after a claim is
  proof.
- **Keep the door image, fix the text around it.** Keep. The copy around it gets
  rewritten to serve whichever beat it lands in after the reorder.
- **Badges stay.** Untouched.
- **Robinhood's own retirement article**
  (`robinhood.com/us/en/learn/articles/how-much-should-i-save-for-retirement/`)
  is a good citation for beat 1: the brokerage publishes education on exactly the
  behaviour FIRE tokenises. Cite it as a link, not as an endorsement.

## The two credibilities, and why they stack

The page has two pieces of borrowed legitimacy available and currently leads
with neither.

- **Sonder's is demand-side.** Millions of people already follow this strategy
  with their own money. It answers "would anyone want this".
- **Vlad's is supply-side.** The chain's CEO named this mechanic as something his
  team never anticipated. It answers "is this real, and is it allowed here".

They are different proofs and they do not substitute for each other. Run the
demand one first, because wanting has to precede believing.

## Risks worth naming

- **The clip is the page's biggest asset and its biggest liability.** Handled as
  above it is fine. Handled loosely it is a false-endorsement claim against a
  listed brokerage.
- **Do not let the movement framing overpromise.** "Financial independence,
  retire early" is a real strategy with real risk; $FIRE is a token. The page
  should align with the movement's *discipline*, never imply it delivers the
  movement's *outcome*.
- **Verify the subreddit numbers are current** before they move above the fold.
  They carry more weight at the top than they did at the bottom, and the existing
  caveat about overlap and reach has to move with them.

## Build order

1. Reorder the existing beats. Movement to the top, receipts after Vlad's slot.

2. Look at it. If the argument does not already read better, the problem is

3. Write beat 2, the pivot.
4. Build beat 3, the quote, with the attribution and disclaimer settled first.
5. Rewrite the copy around the door image to fit its new position.
