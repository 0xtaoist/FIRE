## Why this matters

"Hold a meme coin, get stock tokens airdropped to you" is not an analogy for
FIRE. It is a one-sentence description of the entire product.

The chain's CEO is describing our mechanic as the example of what his own team
did not anticipate. Everything the landing page currently spends its first
screen trying to establish — that this is real, that it is allowed, that it is
native to this chain rather than bolted onto it — he establishes in eleven words.
